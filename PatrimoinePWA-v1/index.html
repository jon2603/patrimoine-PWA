Confirm DEWS credit

A regular employer contribution is due on the 5th of the month.

Confirm only after you have seen the contribution credited to your DEWS account. It will be recorded once for this month and will increase Other / DIFC DEWS Pension in the dashboard.

A coupon or scheduled bond payment is due.

Confirm after you have received the payment. The amount will be credited to the bank holding the bond and recorded once in the Journal.
